# Comprehensive Metric Definitions & KPI Dictionary

**Project:** Retail Sales & Profitability Analytics  
**Scope:** Standardized Enterprise Definitions, Mathematical Formulas, and Edge Case Handling  

---

## 1. Executive Financial Metrics

### 1.1 Total Sales Revenue ($)
- **Mathematical Formula:**  
  $$\text{Total Revenue} = \sum_{i=1}^{N} \text{sales}_i$$
- **Business Meaning:** Gross transaction volume generated by commercial sales after promotional discounts are applied.
- **Denominator / Base:** Evaluated across all verified, non-quarantined order line items ($N = 9,994$).
- **Edge Cases:** If the dataset is empty, returns `$0.00`. If individual line items have `sales <= 0`, they are quarantined during cleaning.
- **Precision:** Rounded to two decimal places (`$#,##0.00`).

### 1.2 Net Contribution Profit ($)
- **Mathematical Formula:**  
  $$\text{Net Profit} = \sum_{i=1}^{N} \text{profit}_i$$
- **Business Meaning:** Net bottom-line dollar return generated after subtracting cost of goods sold and promotional concessions.
- **Characteristics:** Can be positive (profitable) or negative (loss-making transaction).
- **Edge Cases:** If the dataset is empty, returns `$0.00`.
- **Precision:** Rounded to two decimal places (`$#,##0.00`).

### 1.3 Overall Profit Margin Ratio (%)
- **Mathematical Formula:**  
  $$\text{Overall Profit Margin} = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{N} \text{profit}_i}{\sum_{i=1}^{N} \text{sales}_i}$$
- **Business Meaning:** Efficiency ratio indicating how many cents of net profit are generated per dollar of top-line revenue.
- **CRITICAL Denominator Rule:** Must be computed as the ratio of aggregate profit to aggregate revenue ($\frac{\sum \text{profit}}{\sum \text{sales}}$), **NOT** the arithmetic mean of individual line-item margins ($\frac{1}{N}\sum \frac{\text{profit}_i}{\text{sales}_i}$), which introduces severe weighting bias toward small dollar purchases.
- **Edge Cases:** If $\sum \text{sales} == 0$, returns `0.0%` (safe division via `np.where`).
- **Precision:** Expressed as a percentage rounded to two decimal places (`0.00%`).

## 2. Commercial & Operational Metrics

### 2.1 Total Orders (Distinct Order Count)
- **Mathematical Formula:**  
  $$\text{Total Orders} = |\text{Unique}(\{\text{order\_id}_i\}_{i=1}^N)|$$
- **Business Meaning:** Count of distinct customer checkout events. An individual order may contain multiple distinct merchandise line items.
- **Dataset Context:** 9,994 transaction line items aggregate into 5,009 distinct customer orders (an average of 2.0 line items per order).

### 2.2 Average Order Value (AOV in $)
- **Mathematical Formula:**  
  $$\text{AOV} = \frac{\text{Total Sales Revenue}}{\text{Total Unique Orders}} = \frac{\sum \text{sales}}{|\text{Unique}(\text{order\_id})|}$$
- **Business Meaning:** Average gross expenditure committed by a customer during a single checkout transaction.
- **Denominator Rule:** Denominated by unique orders, **never** by raw line item count (which would yield average line item size instead of true basket value).
- **Edge Cases:** Returns `$0.00` if order count is zero.

### 2.3 Loss Transaction Rate (%)
- **Mathematical Formula:**  
  $$\text{Loss Rate} = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{N} \mathbb{I}(\text{profit}_i < 0)}{N} \times 100\%$$
- **Business Meaning:** Percentage of order line items that operate at a negative contribution margin.
- **Target Benchmark:** Ideal operational threshold is `< 12.0%`. Measured baseline is `18.71%`.

### 2.4 Shipping Latency (Fulfillment Duration in Days)
- **Mathematical Formula:**  
  $$\text{Shipping Duration} = \text{ship\_date}_i - \text{order\_date}_i \quad (\text{in elapsed calendar days})$$
- **Business Meaning:** Turnaround time from purchase authorization to carrier dispatch.
- **Validation Rule:** Must be non-negative ($\ge 0$). In the verified dataset, ranges from 0 to 7 days, averaging 3.96 days.

## 3. Growth & Time Series Metrics

### 3.1 Month-over-Month (MoM) Growth (%)
- **Mathematical Formula:**  
  $$\text{MoM Growth}_t = \frac{\text{Metric}_t - \text{Metric}_{t-1}}{|\text{Metric}_{t-1}|} \times 100\%$$
- **Implementation:** Computed using SQLite `LAG(metric) OVER (ORDER BY order_year_month)` or pandas `.shift(1)`.
- **Absolute Value Denominator:** The denominator $|\text{Metric}_{t-1}|$ ensures mathematically consistent growth direction when prior-period profits are negative.
- **Edge Cases:** Period 1 (`2014-01`) produces `NULL` / `NaN`.

### 3.2 Year-over-Year (YoY) Growth (%)
- **Mathematical Formula:**  
  $$\text{YoY Growth}_Y = \frac{\text{Metric}_Y - \text{Metric}_{Y-1}}{\text{Metric}_{Y-1}} \times 100\%$$
- **Business Meaning:** Annual expansion benchmark comparing full 12-month fiscal periods (2014 to 2017).

## 4. Segmentation & Customer RFM Definitions

### 4.1 Promotional Discount Tiers
Transactions are binned into 5 strategic operational brackets:
1. `0% (No Discount)`: Promotional discount $= 0.00$
2. `1% - 10% (Low)`: $0.00 < \text{discount} \le 0.10$
3. `11% - 20% (Moderate)`: $0.10 < \text{discount} \le 0.20$
4. `21% - 40% (High)`: $0.20 < \text{discount} \le 0.40$
5. `> 40% (Deep Discount)`: $\text{discount} > 0.40$

### 4.2 Customer RFM Metrics
- **Recency (R):** Number of days elapsed between the customer's most recent order date and the maximum order date in the dataset (`2017-12-30`).
- **Frequency (F):** Number of distinct orders placed by the customer across the 4-year period.
- **Monetary (M):** Cumulative gross sales spend attributed to the customer account.
- **Customer Lifetime Profit:** Cumulative net profit contributed by the customer.
